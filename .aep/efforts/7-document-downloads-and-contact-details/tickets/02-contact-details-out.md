# feat(site): the contact line without an email, and the email out of every other output

## Outcome
The document's contact line becomes the list the plan's "Interfaces" fixes: two hidden slots for an email and a phone, then the nationality, the location, and the profile URL, with the separator bars moved to the trailing edge of each item so that hiding the first two does not strand a bar. It renders no email address in either variant. The layout footer loses its email line, the home page loses its email action, `src/lib/resume.ts` emits a `basics` with no `email`, and `scripts/readme-profile.mjs` writes a profile block with no email line, with `README.md` regenerated in the same change. `documentHazards` stops proving the contact block is in the document's flow by looking for a `mailto:` and starts looking for `[data-cv-contact]` inside `<main>`.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] No built page, neither `resume.json`, neither rendered PDF, and no line of `README.md` contains the email address or the string `mailto:`, checked by hand on a built tree at this ticket; `pnpm readme --check` passes with the email absent (criterion 2).
- [ ] The CV page, the resume page, and both rendered PDFs show the nationality in the contact line in both languages; the home page, `README.md`, and both `resume.json` documents do not contain it (criterion 5).
- [ ] The contact line renders the slots hidden and the bars correctly with the first two items absent, in both languages and both directions, with no doubled or leading bar, checked on paper in Arabic as well as on screen (criterion 5, criterion 7).
- [ ] `documentHazards` passes over all four document pages with its new proof, and fails when the contact list is moved outside `<main>`, tried once (criterion 7).
- [ ] Both `resume.json` documents still validate against `@jsonresume/schema` and the `jsonResume` check passes (criterion 2).
- [ ] `pnpm test` passes with `tests/home.spec.ts` updated: the footer no longer asserts an email line and the contact actions no longer assert an email action (criterion 8).

## Relevant areas
`src/components/CvDocument.astro` (the `<header>` contact list), `src/layouts/Base.astro` (the footer), `src/pages/[locale]/index.astro` (the contact actions), `src/lib/resume.ts` (`basics`), `scripts/readme-profile.mjs`, `README.md`, `scripts/check-dist.mjs` (`documentHazards`), `src/styles/global.css` (the contact line's bars), `src/lib/i18n.ts` (`home.email` becomes unused), `tests/home.spec.ts`.

## Constraints
- The slot markup, the slot order, and the trailing-bar inversion are the plan's "Interfaces". An implementer who moves an optional item to the end of the line breaks the bar rule, which is why the plan says so there.
- The email is not removed from `src/content/profile.yaml`. What changes is what reads it.
- `README.md` is regenerated by `pnpm readme` and committed in this change, never edited by hand. The moment this merges, the profile page GitHub shows has no email on it.
- This ticket must land before ticket 04 and ticket 05, per the plan's step order: the check in 05 would otherwise be written against a tree that still fails it.
